Highlights of My Project Experience

Deloitte Audit Platform

Omnia is Deloitte’s cloud-based audit platform that uses AI, analytics, and real-time collaboration to deliver efficient, high-quality, and insight-driven audits.

The platform enables auditors across regions to manage audit engagements, working papers, compliance activities, and reporting through a centralized digital ecosystem.

As part of the enablement project, Levvia working papers were integrated into Omnia to ensure seamless workflows, improved compatibility, and standardized audit processes across multiple regions.

DebiCheck

DebiCheck is a secure debit order authentication system used for recurring payments in South Africa.

Transactions included TT1 (real-time messaging), TT2 (batch processing), and TT3 (real-time USSD authentication).

The process covered prepayment (mandate), payment (collection), and post-payment dispute handling.

Payments & Collections

Payments and collections involved AML compliance checks for Cross Border Monetary Area (CBMA) transactions involving Namibia, Eswatini, and Lesotho.

Transactions passed through SAFEWATCH and SWIFT validation using debtor and creditor information to prevent fraudulent activity.

Key benefits included enhanced customer trust, reduced fraud, operational efficiency, and improved cash flow management.
